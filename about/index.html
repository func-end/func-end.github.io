About

Rick Evans

Amateur astronomer, observing and imaging with a 1985 Celestron C8

I'm Rick Evans, an amateur astronomer. I've had my Celestron Super C8 Plus since about 1991. I use it for visual observing and planetary imaging, and in the film days I photographed through it with an Olympus OM-1.

In September 2026 I bought a ZWO Seestar S50 Pro for deep-sky imaging. When the corners of its frames looked soft, I measured it, then measured its replacement the same way. The Seestar S50 Pro tilt measurements are the result. The tools I used are free on GitHub.
